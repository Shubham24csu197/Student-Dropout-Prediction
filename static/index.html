Output

Three-class classification

Dropout, Enrolled, or Graduate, with estimated probabilities for each class.

Important: Predictions are estimates from a machine-learning model, not guarantees or decisions about an individual student. The dataset represents one Portuguese higher-education institution, so results should not be assumed to generalize directly to every university or country. Numerical limits shown here are based on the project's data contract and observed dataset ranges.
